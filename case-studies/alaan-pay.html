The challenge

Alaan issues corporate cards for SMEs in the UAE. It wanted to remove manual work and give customers clearer transactions.

What Lune delivered

Alaan integrated the Enrich API. Each card transaction now shows the merchant name, logo and a detailed category.

The outcome

  • Alaan shipped transaction insights faster.
  • Finance teams see who they paid and for what.

Alaan Pay

The Background

Alaan Pay, a leading smart corporate card provider in the UAE, is transforming how SMEs manage their business expenses. They issue corporate cards to employees for company purchases and automate the invoicing process, bringing digitization to the forefront. For Alaan Pay, it's paramount to stand out in terms of functionality and clarity in user experience.

How did Lune's Transaction Data Enrichment API solve Alaan Pay’s problem?

To eliminate manual bottlenecks and speed up development, Alaan Pay integrated Lune's transaction data enrichment API. This technology changed their development structure by capturing two major technical improvements:

  • Accelerated Development Timeline: The integration significantly accelerated the development timeline for Alaan Pay.
  • Comprehensive Transaction Insights: Lune's engine promptly allowed Alaan Pay to launch a feature delivering comprehensive transaction insights, ranging from brand logos and names to detailed categorization.
