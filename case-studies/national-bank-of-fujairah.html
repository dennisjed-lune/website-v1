How did Lune Insights eliminate manual data processing for NBF?

Before the introduction of Lune, getting a comprehensive picture of user spending habits at NBF was an incredibly time-consuming, operational burden. It required the manual examination of highly unstructured, messy data rows

  • The Turnaround: NBF integrated Lune Insights to automate and optimize this pipeline.
  • The Technology: Harnessing the underlying power of advanced machine learning technology, Lune's solution seamlessly transforms raw user transaction data into a wealth of meaningful, highly structured business insights.

What business and efficiency outcomes did NBF secure with Lune?

By deploying Lune Insights, NBF completely transformed its operational capabilities

  • Tailored Services: NBF can now offer hyper-personalized financial services that are intricately tailored to meet the specific daily needs of their corporate users.
  • Faster Decision-Making: The absolute clarity brought by Lune's data insights fuels significantly quicker and more accurate internal decision-making.
  • Cost Reductions: Improving data processing speeds and operational accuracy has directly led to enhanced banking efficiency and considerable institutional cost reductions.
