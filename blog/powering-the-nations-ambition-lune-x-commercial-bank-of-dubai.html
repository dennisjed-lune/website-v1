Powering the Nation’s Ambition: Lune x Commercial Bank of Dubai

Lune is proud to partner with Commercial Bank of Dubai (CBD) to enhance the personal banking experience through smarter, clearer financial insights.

Published on 22 July 2025

At Lune, we believe that financial literacy and transparency are at the heart of every empowered customer experience. That’s why we’re proud to be one of the partners contributing to the Commercial Bank of Dubai’s (CBD) bold initiative to back the nation’s ambition through cutting-edge digital transformation.

CBD’s 2023 campaign, "Backing the Nation’s Ambition," isn’t just a tagline—it’s a commitment to innovation, inclusion, and progress. As one of the UAE’s leading banks, CBD is setting a new standard for how banks can inspire and serve the next generation of customers. At Lune, we’re thrilled to play a part in bringing that vision to life.

As a financial data platform that transforms raw transaction data into meaningful insights, Lune enables CBD’s personal banking customers to better understand their spending, manage their budgets, and build healthier financial habits—right from their banking app. With our API-powered enrichment engine, CBD is able to provide users with:

  • Cleaned, categorized transactions that make spending transparent and traceable
  • Personalized financial insights that support smarter decision-making
  • Intuitive, real-time summaries that enhance customer engagement and trust

Our partnership with CBD reflects our shared belief in putting the customer first—and using data to empower rather than overwhelm. Whether it’s a young professional trying to track monthly expenses or someone building better financial habits, financial clarity should be simple, secure, and seamlessly integrated.

This collaboration is more than a tech integration—it’s a statement. One that aligns with the UAE’s broader vision for economic growth, digital inclusion, and financial wellness. Together, CBD and Lune are making sure that every customer has the tools they need to thrive in a rapidly changing world.

We’re excited for what’s ahead—and honored to stand alongside CBD and other forward-thinking partners who are building the future of finance in the region.
