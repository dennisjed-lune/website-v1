PDF Bank Statement Parsing: A Game-Changer for Financial Data Management

Imagine the potential of effortless bank statement parsing, a tool that turns complex PDF statements into enriched, structured data. This breakthrough allows end-users to access valuable insights from their financial transactions and make well-informed decisions.

By providing this solution, we're supporting financial institutions in elevating their customers' user experience, empowering them to better manage their finances. PDF Parsing advantages for our clients include:

  • Extract enriched transaction data from bank statements
  • Offering end-users insights into their bank statements
  • Structuring data for simple analysis and processing
  • Enhancing decision-making by minimizing errors
    ‍

Presenting the Feedback Loop: Improved Data Precision and User Experience

We've introduced a new feature that allows your end customers to give feedback on the accuracy and relevance of the enriched transaction data.

With this feedback feature, end-users can provide input on any inaccurately enriched transactions, ensuring that your data remains precise and relevant. We know that dependable financial data is vital, and our feedback feature is one way we guarantee the highest data quality for our users.

The feedback loop enables our clients to:

  • Directly share feedback on enriched transactions in an automated way
  • Enhance customer experience by simplifying the feedback process
  • Get accurate preferences on personal finances
  • Easily analyze and comprehend user feedback
    ‍

Transactions Update Webhook: Seamless Data Management


Our platform ensures a seamless experience for users by updating historically enriched transactions and providing new enriched ones according to the latest updates from our API. This automated process eliminates confusion between old and new data, ensuring that users always have the most up-to-date and accurate transaction information.

Lune's automated update notification system elevates the user experience in several ways:

  • Providing instant notifications to keep users informed of transaction updates
  • Offering detailed tracking of revised transaction data
  • Facilitating seamless updates for both past and new transactions and maintaining the most up-to-date information for users
